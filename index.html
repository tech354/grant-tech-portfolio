How it works

From documents to approval in four steps.

1

Documentation

We prepare and compile business licences, product specs, drawings, manuals, labels and AIR papers for foreign brands.

2

Testing

We pick the right recognised lab, submit samples and track results against the correct IS code.

3

Submission

Test reports, forms and technical data go to BIS or the relevant authority. We answer every officer query.

4

Certificate

Your approval is granted and your product can be sold legally. We also handle renewals and surveillance.
